# Guest 1337: manual in-game checks

Setup: play as Guest 1337 against Slasher. Hotbar: 2 = Block, 3 = Charge, 4 = Punch.

## Passives
- [ ] Max HP is 110.
- [ ] Stand stacked with another survivor in front of the killer. Single-target killer hits pick Guest (priority 2, 3 while blocking).

## Block (slot 2)
- [ ] Press it. RES (V) shows for 1 s, and you move at half speed for 1.75 s. Cooldown is 27 s.
- [ ] Block a Slasher Slash (left-click M1). No damage, no Bleeding, and a "BLOCKED!" flash.
- [ ] After that block: +10 blue overheal, Speed I 2 s, Strength I 5 s, and your speed is back to normal.
- [ ] Also after that block: the killer is slowed (30%) for 1 s, Punch's cooldown resets, and Punch shows PARRY.
- [ ] Block three or more M1s over time. The overheal caps at 30 and does not decay.
- [ ] Block a Behead (an ability). The block breaks: you take 15 instead of 25.
- [ ] After the break: Resistance II for 3 s, and all your abilities are refused ("busy") for about 2 s.
- [ ] One block stops only one attack.
- [ ] After the 1 s window, hits land normally.

## Punch (slot 4)
- [ ] Without a parry: a 0.6 s windup.
- [ ] The killer in front (within ~2.6 blocks) takes 25 damage, Helpless + Slowness I for 2 s, and a knockback. No stun.
- [ ] Only one target is hit. Cooldown is 55 s.
- [ ] Parry Punch, within 3 s of a successful block: Resistance II during a 0.4 s windup.
- [ ] Parry Punch hit: 42 damage with the block's Strength I, a 2 s stun (2.6 s with the killer's recovery), and a larger knockback.
- [ ] Parry Punch hits everyone in the arc.
- [ ] The parry expires after 3 s.

## Charge (slot 3)
- [ ] Press it. A 1.5 s shoulder dash in the facing direction, with slight steering.
- [ ] Stamina does not regenerate during the dash.
- [ ] Hit the killer: 5 damage (6 with Strength) and a knockback that is stronger the longer you charged before impact. You get Resistance III for 0.5 s.
- [ ] Charge the same killer again within 20 s. Nothing happens and there is no penalty. The slot shows the lockout seconds.
- [ ] Miss (time out or wall): Slowness II for 3×distance(studs)/13 seconds, clamped to 3–15 s.
- [ ] Sneak at a generator mid-charge. The charge stops with no penalty.
- [ ] Cooldown is 40 s.
