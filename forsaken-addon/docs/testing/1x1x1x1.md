# 1x1x1x1: manual test

Setup: open the Forsaken Menu, pick **1x1x1x1** as the killer, add 4+ bot survivors and start. Slot 1 = Slash, 2 = Mass Infection, 3 = Entanglement, 4 = Unstable Eye, 5 = Rejuvenate the Rotten. Note: HP is 102,550 (wiki value). `config.oneXOneHpOverride` changes it.

## Slash (slot 1)
- [ ] Hit a survivor. They lose **20** and get **Glitched I** (their HP/stamina HUD scrambles) and **Poisoned I for 5 s**.

## Mass Infection (slot 2)
- [ ] Press it. There is a loud windup sound, and for **1.7 s** you can barely move (20% speed, no sprint).
- [ ] Survivors within about 5 blocks of you, **including behind you**, take **55** (30 + 25). They get **Glitched II 5 s**, **Poisoned V 3 s**, and a short speed boost.
- [ ] A wide green/purple shockwave (about 14 blocks wide) then rushes forward **through walls**. Survivors it reaches take **30** with **Glitched I + Poisoned I for 5 s**. Survivors who were already Glitched take **+10**.
- [ ] Everyone hit is revealed to you for **12 s**, colored by their health.
- [ ] Survivors on higher ground than the wave are not hit.
- [ ] A stun during the windup cancels the ability. Being stunned while the wave travels makes it harmless during the stun.
- [ ] Your own zombies are never hurt by it.

## Entanglement (slot 3)
- [ ] Press it. After **0.75 s** you throw a sword (purple trail) that flies about 27 blocks and stops at walls.
- [ ] On hit, the survivor takes **10**, is revealed for **12 s**, gets **Helpless + Glitched II for 6 s** and **Slowness X for 1 s** (frozen), and is yanked toward you. The pull is 50% stronger if they were already Glitched.
- [ ] As the survivor: a "JUMP to close pop-ups (12)" bar appears. Each jump closes one pop-up.
- [ ] Throw it at one of your zombies. The zombie dies instantly and you get **Speed I for 8 s**.

## Unstable Eye (slot 4)
- [ ] At round start the item is on a **12.5 s** cooldown.
- [ ] Press it. You can walk but not sprint during the **1.5 s** startup and for 0.35 s after it.
- [ ] Then every survivor's aura shows for **8 s**, colored by health (green/yellow/red). You get **Speed II for 1.5 s** and **Blindness III for 7 s**, and the screen flashes red. Stamina starts regenerating immediately.

## Rejuvenate the Rotten (slot 5)
- [ ] Before any kill, pressing it says "no charge".
- [ ] Kill a survivor. The HUD shows `x1` (it never goes above 1).
- [ ] Press it. The ritual takes **3.5 s** and a stun does not cancel it. A zombie ("Rotten <name>", green aura only you can see) rises where each killed survivor died.
- [ ] Zombies wander. They chase survivors who come within about 7 blocks (20 studs) and give up beyond about 11 blocks (30 studs). They ignore Undetectable survivors.
- [ ] A zombie's touch gives **Poisoned I + Glitched I for 8 s** and reveals the survivor to you for 8 s. A survivor who was already Glitched also takes **5** damage per touch (every 1.5 s). Only Glitched survivors can be killed by zombies.
- [ ] Survivors can kill zombies (**25 HP**). Two of your Slashes also kill one and give you **Speed I for 8 s**.
- [ ] With all corpses already standing as zombies, pressing it says "no corpses".
