# Jane Doe: manual in-game checks

Setup: `/scriptevent forsaken:quickstart survivor jane_doe`. Hotbar: 2 = Crystal Pitch, 3 = Hatchet. The Crystal Pitch slot shows your Shatterpoint (SHP); the Hatchet slot shows the killer's Resonance level.

## Shatterpoint (passive)
- [ ] You start with 20 SHP (blue extra HP) on top of 60 HP.
- [ ] A 20-damage killer hit removes only 12 SHP, and your HP stays at 60.
- [ ] Bleeding eats SHP at full rate (no 40% reduction).
- [ ] While SHP absorbs a hit, you are not kicked off a generator.
- [ ] Medkits and Dispensers do not refill SHP. Only your landed abilities do, up to 30.
- [ ] Lose all SHP. "Shatterpoint broken (40s)" flashes, your abilities give no SHP, and after 40 s you get 5 SHP back.
- [ ] With two Jane Does alive, SHP gains are 10% smaller.

## Digital Footprint (passive)
- [ ] Watch the killer move. Only you see a trail of footprints, red first, then yellow, then grey, gone after about 6 s.
- [ ] Other survivors do not see them. A killer standing still leaves no new prints.

## Crystal Pitch (slot 2, charge)
- [ ] Press once to start charging. You are 30% slower. Press again to throw, or it throws by itself after 2 s.
- [ ] Longer charge means a faster, longer throw and a bigger blast. Look up to throw farther.
- [ ] The crystal arcs and explodes on anyone, a wall or the floor.
- [ ] Blast on survivors: PURE on their HUD for 3 s, and you get +2 SHP (once per throw).
- [ ] Blast on the killer: RESON I on the killer for 30 s, +3 SHP, and the killer sees your outline for 3 s.
- [ ] Hit the killer again while it has Resonance. II, then III (max).
- [ ] A direct hit from 110+ studs (about 40 blocks) gives Resonance II at once.
- [ ] Hit the killer within 10 s of a stun, or within 10 s after its Resonance ended. No Resonance, but you still get +3 SHP.
- [ ] The 16 s cooldown starts on the throw.

## Hatchet (slot 3)
- [ ] Press it. A 0.75 s windup with RES I, then a short lunge (about 12 studs) and a single hit.
- [ ] Killer without Resonance: 25 damage, Helpless and Slowness II for 2 s, +5 SHP.
- [ ] Killer with Resonance I / II / III: stun 2.5 / 3.5 / 4.5 s, +10 / 20 / 30 SHP, and the Resonance is used up.
- [ ] Killer stun-immune (just stunned): damage only, no SHP, and its Resonance stays.
- [ ] Against John Doe: only 10 damage.
- [ ] Cooldown is 35 s.
