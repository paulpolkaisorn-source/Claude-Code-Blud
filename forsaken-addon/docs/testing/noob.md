# Noob: manual in-game checks

Setup: play as Noob against any killer (a bot killer is fine). Hotbar: 2 = Bloxy Cola, 3 = Slateskin Potion, 4 = Ghostburger.

## Bloxy Cola (slot 2)
- [ ] Press it. A drink sound plays, you move about 10% slower, and you cannot sprint for 2.5 s.
- [ ] After the drink: SPD I shows on the HUD for 10 s. Any Slowness you had is removed. Cooldown is 50 s.
- [ ] Get stunned while drinking. You get no speed.
- [ ] With the cola speed active, take a Slowness hit (e.g. Dusekkar's beam or a killer slow). You lose the speed and do not get slowed.
- [ ] Use it during Slateskin. Only a "boowomp" plays, you get nothing, and the 50 s cooldown still starts.
- [ ] While eating or drinking something else, pressing it is refused ("busy").

## Slateskin Potion (slot 3, toggle)
- [ ] Press it. You drink for 1.5 s at -20% speed with no sprint, and the slot shows ACTIVE.
- [ ] After the drink: SLATE on the HUD, 120 overheal (blue HP), and about -45% speed.
- [ ] The overheal drains slowly (1 HP per 0.5 s).
- [ ] A killer hit takes overheal before HP and causes no Bleeding.
- [ ] You cannot gain Speed, Poison or Burning while Slateskin is on.
- [ ] Stand stacked with another survivor. A single-target killer hit picks Noob first.
- [ ] Press again within 2.5 s. It is refused with a message.
- [ ] Press again after 2.5 s. Slateskin and its overheal end, you get Speed I for 2 s, and the 46 s cooldown starts.
- [ ] Let it run out (6 s). Speed II for 2 s, then the 46 s cooldown.
- [ ] Recast also works while Helpless.
- [ ] Get stunned while drinking. You get no Slateskin, but the cooldown starts.

## Ghostburger (slot 4)
- [ ] Press it. You eat for 2 s at -20% speed with no sprint.
- [ ] After eating: you are nearly invisible (vanilla Invisibility) for 10 s.
- [ ] The killer's aura reads do not show you (Undetectable).
- [ ] Cooldown is 40 s.
