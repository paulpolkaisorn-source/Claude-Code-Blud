# Dusekkar: manual in-game checks

Setup: `/scriptevent forsaken:quickstart survivor dusekkar` (bot teammates are your allies). Hotbar: 2 = Spawn Protection, 3 = Plasma Beam.

## Levitation (passive)
- [ ] Your footsteps make no sound. Against John Doe, his trail does not affect you.

## Spawn Protection (slot 2, charge)
- [ ] Look toward a teammate within 95 studs (walls do not matter) and press it. A blue beam links you, the teammate gets RES IV, and you are 30% slower.
- [ ] Nobody under the crosshair: "No ally to shield" and no cooldown.
- [ ] The shield lasts up to 3.5 s, or press again to stop. The 35 s cooldown starts when it ends.
- [ ] A hit that would kill the teammate is cancelled completely. "Spawn Protection saved you!" shows, and the shield ends.
- [ ] It is used before Two Time's second life.
- [ ] Keep a wall between you for more than 2 s. The shield collapses ("line of sight").
- [ ] Move more than 95 studs apart. It collapses.
- [ ] Shield the same teammate again within 30 s. Refused (no cooldown spent). Another teammate works.
- [ ] Three Dusekkars shielding at the same time: all shields shatter and each gets Blindness III + Slowness III for 6 s.

## Plasma Beam (slot 3)
- [ ] Press it. A 2 s windup at -25% speed, then a straight beam (75 studs) that passes through walls.
- [ ] Killer in the beam: Slowness I 4 s.
- [ ] Teammate in the beam: Speed I 3 s and 25 blue overheal that drains 1 per 0.5 s. Only the first teammate in line gets it.
- [ ] The beam can hit both the killer and one teammate.
- [ ] With two Dusekkars alive: 3.5 s Slowness and 22.5 overheal.
- [ ] Cooldown is 28 s.
