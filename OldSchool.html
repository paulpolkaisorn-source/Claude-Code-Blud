        Put it in Roblox Studio

        1. Download the model

          Save OldSchool.rbxm on your PC. It holds the whole map as normal anchored parts.

        2. Clear the template

          In a new place, delete the Baseplate and SpawnLocation in Workspace. The baseplate sits right where the school's ground goes.

        3. Insert it

          Right-click Workspace in the Explorer, choose Insert from File… and pick OldSchool.rbxm. It lands centred at the origin, ready to play. Move the RealisticLighting script into ServerScriptService for the dusk fog look.

        Prefer a script? Paste the script below into Studio's View → Command Bar and press Enter. It builds the same map, and you can change SEED for a different mess, or edit colours and rooms first.
