## Game defects

5 check(s) failed. Diagnosed defects are listed first; each failed check stays in the suite.

### DEF-01 (high): Brawler preview canvas is force-context-lost on match start and reused by the next select screen

- Failing checks: D03, D04, D05, D06, D19
- Evidence: Reproduced in a fresh page with real clicks: after START, the preview canvas (class preview-canvas) reports isContextLost() === true. Stack of the loseContext() call (runtime, served paths): toMatchmaking (src/game.js:549) > closePreview (src/game.js:539) > preview.dispose (src/entities/preview.js:89) > WebGLRenderer.forceContextLoss (three.module.js). The next visit to the select screen builds a new WebGLRenderer on the same canvas. The canvas returns the lost context, so three.js WebGLCapabilities throws "TypeError: Cannot read properties of null (reading 'precision')" in getMaxPrecision. Seen on every select-screen visit after a match: checks D03, D04, D05 and D06 each record one page error at the select step, while the match flow itself still reaches playing.
- Impact: Uncaught page error on each select-screen visit after the first match. The brawler preview renderer cannot be constructed on those visits, so the turntable does not render. Per the WebGL spec a lost context stays lost on its canvas, so this does not depend on the GPU.
- Suggested fix: Do not force-lose the context of a canvas that will be reused. Either keep one preview renderer for the page lifetime and only pause its loop, or create a fresh canvas for each preview and dispose the old one completely.

## Known limits of this suite

- Perf numbers are SwiftShader numbers from a 4-vCPU container. Use them for quality-to-quality comparison only.
- Supers, dash, totems, walls, bush visibility and bot movement are verified through game state and bus events (`window.__BRAWL__`), not pixels. Audio and visual polish are not checked.
- Touch taps use Playwright's touch tap. In exploratory runs (outside this suite), raw CDP touchStart/touchEnd pairs were unreliable on the pads: the attack pad fired on 3 of 12 presses (0-400 ms) and the super pad on 1 of 10, while Playwright taps fired on 16 of 16 (attack) and 2 of 2 (super). The cause (game touch handler or CDP injection) is not confirmed; verify on a real touch device before filing it as a game defect.
- The left-stick drag uses raw CDP touchStart/touchMove/touchEnd and passed in every run (M04).
- Results and flows were checked on the 1280x720 and 390x844 viewports only.
