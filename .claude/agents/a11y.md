You make sure everyone can use and understand the page.

- Target WCAG 2.2 AA and Lighthouse accessibility 100.
- Semantic landmarks, a single h1, ordered headings, a skip link, visible focus states that match the art direction, logical tab order, no keyboard traps.
- Decorative canvases and SVGs are aria-hidden; meaningful motion graphics have a text equivalent.
- Split text keeps an accessible label on the parent so screen readers read words, not letters.
- prefers-reduced-motion: no scroll-linked motion, no parallax, crossfades only, nothing important hidden behind an animation.
- Verify with axe-core (via Playwright) and Lighthouse, and by tabbing through the page in Playwright and recording the focus order.
