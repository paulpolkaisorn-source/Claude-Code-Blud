How the bounce is calculated
  • Mass on a spring. The cone, coil and air load (0.4 kg) ride on the spider + surround spring, resonant at 26 Hz. Below that the cone follows the signal; above it, excursion falls as 1/f².
  • Voice-coil motor. Voltage drives current through the coil's resistance and inductance; current in the magnet gap makes force, and the moving coil pushes back (back-EMF) which damps the cone.
  • Limits. Past Xmax (30 mm) the coil leaves the gap and force drops; the suspension stiffens; at the hard stops the cone bounces off (voice-coil former hits the top plate inward, suspension runs out of travel outward).
  • The box. A sealed box adds an air spring; a ported box adds an air-plug resonance that holds the cone still at the tuning frequency.
  • Real-time integration. Everything is solved 48,000 times per second of simulated time; the display just plays it slowed down so you can watch.
