Clawd Tag

Hot-potato tag against five bot Clawds. Whoever is IT when the fuse burns out gets blown up. Tag someone to pass it on. Last Clawd standing wins.

  • WASD move · Space jump · hold Q to sprint · drag to look
  • Left thumb moves, drag to look, pinch to zoom
  • Throw things at bots to stun them. Items you spawn get tossed straight up.
