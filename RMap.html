R Map

A blocky but realistic Roblox map shaped like the letter R: grass, concrete walls on a red-brick base, wood, glass, trees and Roblox landmarks, with a loop of play areas inside. Tap an area below to fly the camera there.

        Put it in Roblox Studio

        1. Download the model

          Save RMap.rbxm on your PC. It holds the whole map as normal parts.

        2. Clear the template

          In a new place, delete the Baseplate and SpawnLocation in Workspace. The baseplate sits right where the R's floor goes.

        3. Insert it

          Right-click Workspace in the Explorer, choose Insert from File… and pick RMap.rbxm. It lands centred at the origin, ready to play.

        Prefer a script? Paste the script below into Studio's View → Command Bar and press Enter. It builds the same map, and you can edit SHAPE, colours and areas first.
